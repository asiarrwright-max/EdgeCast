1. Recalibrate forecast uncertainty using real forecast error

* The Open-Meteo forecast that was actually available at the prediction timestamp
* The verified final observation from the exact GHCND/Kalshi settlement station
* Forecast lead time
* City/station
* Season or month when enough data exists

Store the signed error and absolute error for every observation.

Use those verified errors to calculate sigma and bias. Until a bucket has enough observations, use a conservative prior or pooled fallback rather than an unrealistically small sigma.

* Minimum observations required before a learned value is used
* Fallback hierarchy
* Sigma floor and ceiling
