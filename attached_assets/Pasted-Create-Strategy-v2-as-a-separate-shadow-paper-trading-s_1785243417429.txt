Create Strategy v2 as a separate shadow paper-trading strategy. Do not modify Strategy v1, its historical trades, or its prediction logic.

The goal of Strategy v2 is to use historical forecast performance and observed weather data to produce better-calibrated probabilities.

Requirements:

1. Preserve Strategy v1
- Strategy v1 must continue operating unchanged.
- Do not overwrite or recalculate any existing v1 predictions or trades.
- Every v2 prediction and paper trade must be labeled strategy_version = "v2".
- Allow analytics to compare v1 and v2 side by side.

2. Historical data model
Create storage for:
- city
- official settlement station
- forecast provider
- forecast timestamp
- forecast lead time
- forecast temperature
- actual observed official temperature
- forecast error
- market threshold or temperature range
- predicted probability
- final contract result
- month and season

3. Historical forecast-error analysis
For each station and forecast lead-time bucket, calculate:
- mean forecast error
- median forecast error
- mean absolute error
- standard deviation of forecast error
- sample size
- seasonal or monthly bias when enough data exists

Do not use a station-specific adjustment when the sample is too small. Fall back to a broader city-level or global estimate and clearly label the fallback.

4. Strategy v2 probability engine
Use:
- current forecast
- historical station-specific forecast bias
- historical forecast-error distribution
- forecast lead time
- historical climatology as a secondary prior
- market threshold or range
- probability calibration based on previous predictions and actual outcomes

Do not treat historical average temperature as more important than the current forecast.

5. Calibration
Track whether predicted probability buckets match actual results.

For example:
- predictions of 10–20%
- actual YES frequency
- calibration adjustment
- sample size

Do not apply aggressive calibration changes when the sample size is small.

6. Shadow paper trading
Run v1 and v2 independently on the same eligible markets.
Each strategy should create its own simulated trade decision.
Do not place real trades.

Show when:
- both strategies trade
- only v1 trades
- only v2 trades
- the strategies choose different sides
- the strategies assign materially different probabilities

7. V2 trade-quality rules
Initially flag or exclude from the primary realistic-results view:
- 1-cent contracts
- zero-volume markets
- missing liquidity
- stale quotes
- large bid/ask spreads
- unsupported or uncertain settlement rules
- missing settlement station
- markets that cannot reasonably be filled at the displayed price

Keep excluded opportunities visible in a separate research view.

8. Backtesting
If archived forecasts available at the historical timestamp can be obtained, backtest v2 using only information that would have been known at that time.

Do not use today’s reconstructed forecast as though it were the original historical forecast.
Do not allow future information or actual outcomes to leak into the prediction.

Clearly distinguish:
- true archived-forecast backtests
- climatology-only historical analysis
- live forward paper trading

9. Comparison dashboard
Compare v1 and v2 using:
- settled trade count
- win rate
- raw ROI
- realistic adjusted ROI
- Brier score
- calibration error
- performance by city
- performance by price bucket
- performance by edge bucket
- performance by lead time
- cumulative P/L

10. Implementation process
Before coding:
- inspect the existing probability and paper-trading architecture
- provide a concise implementation plan
- identify historical data sources and limitations
- identify any database migrations

Do not reset or delete existing data.
Do not connect real-money trading.
