Build a new standalone tab in EdgeCast called:

BET WATCH

PURPOSE
Bet Watch is a decision-support layer for me as a human user.

I want EdgeCast to tell me, in normal language, which Kalshi weather contracts look most attractive RIGHT NOW based on the best information EdgeCast currently has, even when those opportunities have not yet passed every requirement necessary to become an OFFICIAL Forward Test B paper trade.

This is NOT permission for EdgeCast to place real trades.

This is NOT a modification to Forward Test B.

Do not change any forecasting models, calibration, probability calculations, eligibility rules, Guard 8, freshness requirements, paper-trading logic, stake sizing, Forward Test B timestamps, or existing audit behavior.

Bet Watch must be observational/read-only with respect to the existing trading system.

==================================================
1. CREATE A SEPARATE BET WATCH TAB
==================================================

Add “Bet Watch” as its own top-level navigation destination.

Do NOT put this inside Mission Control, Audit & Validation, or another existing page.

Design it mobile-first.

The page should answer one question immediately:

“What does EdgeCast think is the best Kalshi weather bet I could consider right now?”

Do not make me interpret engineering terminology to answer that question.

==================================================
2. TOP CARD: BEST BET RIGHT NOW
==================================================

At the top of Bet Watch, create a prominent card:

BEST BET RIGHT NOW

If EdgeCast currently sees an attractive opportunity, show:

- City
- Kalshi contract / ticker
- Contract question in plain English
- YES or NO
- Current Kalshi ask price
- EdgeCast estimated probability
- Estimated edge
- Model/version producing the estimate
- Forecast temperature / relevant weather value
- Contract threshold or range
- Market settlement date
- Time remaining before market close/settlement when available
- Quote age / last market update
- Data freshness
- Confidence level
- Why EdgeCast likes it
- What could make the recommendation change
- Whether it currently qualifies as an OFFICIAL FTB trade

Most importantly, include a plain-English recommendation such as:

“EdgeCast’s best preliminary opportunity right now is YES on Chicago reaching 88°F or higher. Kalshi is pricing YES at 41¢ while EdgeCast estimates the probability at 57%, giving it an estimated 16 percentage-point edge.”

That is illustrative only. Use REAL LIVE DATA.

Never fabricate a recommendation if no qualifying live opportunity exists.

If nothing is attractive, explicitly say:

“EdgeCast does not see a bet worth taking right now.”

==================================================
3. PRELIMINARY BETS VS OFFICIAL TRADES
==================================================

Bet Watch must deliberately allow me to see promising opportunities BEFORE they become OFFICIAL Forward Test B trades.

Create clear statuses such as:

WATCHING
PRELIMINARY
NEAR OFFICIAL
OFFICIAL-ELIGIBLE
AVOID / STALE

For every candidate, show exactly why it has its current status.

Example:

PRELIMINARY

“EdgeCast sees a strong model/market disagreement, but this quote is currently too old to satisfy the 300-second OFFICIAL Forward Test B freshness rule.”

This distinction is extremely important.

A candidate failing an FTB eligibility guard does NOT automatically mean it is a bad prediction.

Bet Watch should tell me whether the failure is related to:

- stale quote
- no valid ask
- insufficient liquidity
- missing market data
- station verification
- settlement integrity
- model confidence
- insufficient edge
- other eligibility rule

Translate these into normal language.

==================================================
4. DO NOT WEAKEN FORWARD TEST B
==================================================

Bet Watch must consume existing data without modifying Forward Test B.

In particular:

DO NOT change the 300-second freshness threshold.

DO NOT change FTB eligibility rules.

DO NOT turn PRELIMINARY recommendations into OFFICIAL trades.

DO NOT write Bet Watch selections into the OFFICIAL paper-trade population unless the existing trading engine independently qualifies them.

Forward Test B must remain statistically comparable from beginning to end.

==================================================

==================================================

Bet Watch should use the freshest Kalshi market information EdgeCast has available.

Where supported by the existing Kalshi integration, display:

- current YES bid/ask
- current NO bid/ask
- last price
- quote timestamp
- quote age
- volume

- market status

6. RANK OPPORTUNITIES
==================================================

Below Best Bet Right Now, add:

TOP OPPORTUNITIES RIGHT NOW

Rank the best current weather-market opportunities.

Do not rank solely by raw model edge.

The ranking should consider the information already available to EdgeCast, including:

- estimated model edge
- probability confidence
- market liquidity
- quote freshness
- model/data freshness
- station verification
- settlement integrity
- contract type
- forecast uncertainty
- proximity to threshold
- time until market close
- whether multiple EdgeCast model versions agree

Prefer opportunities that are actually actionable over giant theoretical edges in dead or stale markets.

Show approximately the top 5 opportunities by default.

==================================================
7. PLAIN-ENGLISH “WHY THIS BET?”
==================================================

Every recommendation needs a short explanation understandable without knowing EdgeCast internals.

Example:

WHY EDGECAST LIKES THIS

“The latest forecast puts Dallas near 101°F. Kalshi is pricing the chance of exceeding 100°F substantially lower than EdgeCast’s model. The market is active and the quote is fresh, so this is currently one of the cleaner disagreements EdgeCast sees.”

Also show:

WHAT TO WATCH

Example:

“The forecast is close to the contract boundary, so a 1°F forecast shift could materially change this recommendation.”

Do not overstate certainty.

==================================================
8. SHOW ME WHEN TO WAIT
==================================================

Bet Watch should be capable of recommending WAIT.

Examples:

“Good signal, bad market right now. Wait for a fresh Kalshi quote.”

“EdgeCast likes YES, but liquidity is too thin right now.”

“Promising opportunity, but the forecast is too close to the threshold.”

“Strong disagreement, but the underlying market data is stale.”

“Nothing compelling right now.”

A WAIT recommendation is preferable to forcing a bet.

If appropriate, use model agreement as an additional confidence signal.

Do NOT alter the underlying models as part of this task.
