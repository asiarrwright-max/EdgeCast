EdgeCast Phase 3A — Automated Paper Trading
EdgeCast can now collect Kalshi weather markets, calculate EdgeCast probabilities for supported markets, compare them with market-implied probabilities, assign confidence levels, and store prediction snapshots.
Build a focused paper-trading system so we can test whether EdgeCast’s identified probability differences perform well over time.
Priority order:
1. Accurate trade simulation
2. Immutable historical records
3. Correct settlement and profit/loss calculations
4. Automated testing
5. Minimal usable interface
Do not redesign the application.
Do not place real trades.
Do not connect to Kalshi trading endpoints.
Do not request trading API credentials.
Do not add bankroll recommendations, notifications, machine learning, or additional weather categories.
This phase must remain entirely simulated.
## Objective
Whenever EdgeCast analyzes a supported market, determine whether it would hypothetically:
- Buy YES
- Buy NO
- Skip
Record that decision as a paper-trading observation.
After Kalshi settles the market, update the record with the actual result and hypothetical profit or loss.
The purpose is to evaluate EdgeCast objectively over several weeks without risking real money.

## 2. Paper-Trade Eligibility
A market is eligible for a paper trade only when:
- Analysis status is ANALYZED
- EdgeCast probability is available
- Market price is available
- Confidence is High or Very High
- Absolute probability difference is at least 10 percentage points
- The market is still open
- The market has not already received a paper trade under the same strategy version
Default configuration:
- Minimum edge: 10 percentage points
- Minimum confidence: High
- Simulated stake: $10
- Paper trading enabled: true
Make these values configurable later through a simple administrator-only settings section, but use safe defaults first.
If a market does not qualify, record it as skipped only if doing so does not create excessive duplicate records. Store the skip reason.
Example skip reasons:
- Unsupported market
- Confidence too low
- Edge below threshold
- Market price unavailable
- Market already closed
- Existing paper trade
- Forecast unavailable
## 3. Trade Direction
Use a transparent decision rule.
### Buy YES
Create a simulated YES trade when:
EdgeCast probability minus YES purchase probability is greater than or equal to the minimum edge.
Use the actual simulated purchase price available at the decision timestamp.
Prefer YES ask when available because that represents the price to buy YES.
### Buy NO
Create a simulated NO trade when:
EdgeCast probability is sufficiently below the market’s implied YES probability.
Compare EdgeCast’s estimated NO probability:
1 - EdgeCast YES probability
against the available NO ask price.
Use NO ask as the simulated purchase price when available.
Do not infer NO purchase price simply as 100 minus YES ask when a real NO ask is available.
### Skip
Skip when neither side meets the configured threshold.
Store:
- Selected direction
- Purchase price source
- Edge used for the decision
- Reason for the decision
- Strategy version

## 5. Simulated Position Calculation
Use a fixed dollar stake, default $10.
Kalshi-style contracts pay $1 per winning contract and $0 per losing contract.
Calculate simulated quantity using:
stake divided by contract purchase price
Keep full precision internally.
For a winning trade:
profit = total payout minus simulated stake
For a losing trade:
profit = negative simulated stake
Clearly document the assumptions.
Do not include fees, slippage, partial fills, or liquidity assumptions in Phase 3A unless they are already reliably available.
Display a warning that paper results may overstate real-world results because fills, spreads, fees, and liquidity are not modeled.
## 6. Automatic Paper-Trade Creation
After every successful collection and probability-calculation run:
1. Review all eligible analyzed markets.
2. Apply the current paper-trading rules.
3. Create new qualifying paper trades.
4. Avoid duplicates.
5. Record skipped candidates with reasons in job-level statistics or a lightweight decision log.
6. Continue processing if one market fails.
Manual “Run Collection Now” should also trigger this process.
Add paper-trading counts to the job record:
- candidates reviewed
- trades created
- YES trades
- NO trades
- skipped
- errors
## 7. Settlement Processing
Create a scheduled settlement-check job.
For open paper trades:
- Query the official Kalshi market data for current status and settlement result.
- When a market has settled, determine whether YES or NO won.
- Update the paper trade.
- Calculate gross payout, simulated profit/loss, and return percentage.
- Store the settlement timestamp and source.
Do not determine settlement from the weather forecast.
Do not fabricate a result.
Kalshi settlement data should be authoritative when available.
If the market is canceled or voided:
- mark the trade VOID
- return the simulated stake
- set profit/loss to $0
If settlement cannot be verified:
- keep the trade open or mark an explicit verification error
- do not guess

## 10. Duplicate Prevention
Do not create multiple identical paper trades during repeated collection runs.
Default behavior:
- One trade per market ticker per strategy version.
If a market already has a paper trade:
- do not create another
- record the reason as Existing paper trade
Design the schema so future strategies could coexist using different strategy-version identifiers.
