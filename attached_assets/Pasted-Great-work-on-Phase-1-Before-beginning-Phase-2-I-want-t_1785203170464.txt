## 1. Kalshi Market Collection

When "Run Collection Now" is pressed:

- Retrieve all active Kalshi weather markets from the official Kalshi public API.
- Save every retrieved market into PostgreSQL.
- Record:
  - Market ticker
  - Event ticker
  - Market title
  - City
  - Weather market type
  - Open time
  - Close time
  - YES price
  - NO price
  - Volume
  - Collection timestamp

## 2. Weather Collection

For every successfully collected market:

- Match the market to a weather location.
- Retrieve the appropriate Open-Meteo forecast.
- Save the forecast.
- Record whether weather matching succeeded or failed.

Do not silently ignore failed matches.
