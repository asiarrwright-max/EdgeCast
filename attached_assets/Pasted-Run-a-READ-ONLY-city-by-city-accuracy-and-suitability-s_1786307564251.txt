Run a READ-ONLY city-by-city accuracy and suitability study for EdgeCast, then publish the findings into the existing Audit & Validation tab so they remain visible for later review.

Do not change model logic.
Do not change probability formulas.
Do not change calibration.
Do not change Forward Test B.
Do not change eligibility rules.

GOAL

Determine whether EdgeCast would likely become more accurate and more practically useful if we specialized on ONE city, and identify the strongest single-city candidate based on actual historical data.

Use all relevant persisted historical data available in EdgeCast, including settled paper trades, forecast verification/error data, settlement-station metadata, quote/liquidity history, model version results, and market availability.

- % rejected for station verification
- % rejected for liquidity/price floor
- unique market days observed
- unique market tickers
- approximate opportunities per day

If a metric cannot be computed from stored data, show UNKNOWN rather than guessing.

====================================
2. SAMPLE-SIZE QUALITY
====================================

Classify each city’s evidence strength:

VERY LOW
LOW
MODERATE
GOOD
STRONG

Base this on actual sample size and data completeness.

Do not rank a city highly just because it has a good win rate on a tiny sample.

Explicitly flag:
- cities with fewer than 10 settled observations
- cities with fewer than 30 forecast-verification observations
- cities where one or two trades dominate P&L
- cities with materially imbalanced YES/NO exposure

====================================
3. FORECAST ACCURACY
====================================

For each city, evaluate forecast quality separately from trading performance.

Report:

- mean absolute temperature error
- median absolute error
- signed bias
- RMSE if available
- sigma / dispersion
- % within ±1°F
- % within ±2°F
- % within ±3°F
- performance by lead time:
  - <6h
  - 6–12h
  - 12–24h
  - 24–48h
  - 48h+

If possible, separate forecast-error statistics by source label so GHCND/NWS, ERA5, and legacy sources are not mixed incorrectly.

====================================
4. MARKET QUALITY / LIQUIDITY
====================================

For each city, measure how usable the Kalshi market tends to be.

Report:

- % scans with valid ask
- % scans with fresh ask ≤300 sec
- % scans with no usable quote
- average available quantity if stored
- median available quantity if stored
- average time from usable signal to close
- number of distinct market days with at least one actionable quote

If volume/open interest are not stored, say so.

Identify cities where model accuracy may be good but the market is too illiquid to be useful.

====================================
5. MODEL CONSISTENCY
====================================

Compare available model versions by city where possible.

For each city, report:

- V2.2 performance
- V2.3 performance
- V3 performance
- whether versions generally agree on direction
- whether one version materially outperforms others
- whether differences are large enough to be meaningful given sample size

Do not assume newest = best.

If cross-version pairing is not available, say so.

====================================
6. CITY SPECIALIZATION SCORE
====================================

Build a transparent SINGLE-CITY SUITABILITY SCORE.

Do not use a black-box score.

Show the component weights.

Suggested categories:

- forecast accuracy: 30%
- historical calibration/trading quality: 25%
- market liquidity/quote availability: 20%
- sample size/data quality: 15%
- settlement/station integrity: 10%

You may adjust weights slightly if the data clearly justifies it, but document them.

For each city show:
- component scores
- total score
- confidence in score

====================================
7. BEST SINGLE CITY
====================================

Answer plainly:

“If EdgeCast specialized on exactly one city today, which city should it be?”

Return:
- #1 city
- #2 backup
- #3 backup

For the #1 city explain in normal language:

- why it is the strongest candidate
- what EdgeCast does well there
- what remains uncertain
- whether enough evidence exists to justify actual specialization now
- whether focusing on that city could plausibly improve accuracy
- whether it would reduce trade volume too much
- how long validation would likely take using only that city

Do not overstate certainty.
