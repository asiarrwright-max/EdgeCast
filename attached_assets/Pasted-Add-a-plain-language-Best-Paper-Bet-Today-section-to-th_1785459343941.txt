Add a plain-language “Best Paper Bet Today” section to the Strategy Comparison page and, if appropriate, the main dashboard.

This remains paper trading and must never place or recommend a real-money order automatically.

Select no more than one best opportunity from current Kalshi weather markets.

Eligibility requirements:

- The market must be open
- The quote must be fresh
- The opportunity must be executable
- A valid YES or NO ask price must exist
- Station mapping must be verified
- The market must not already be effectively resolved by observed same-day weather
- It must not be marked TEST_EXCLUDED, NON_EXECUTABLE, observation-only, stale, duplicate, or unsupported
- Net edge after estimated fees must meet the configured minimum threshold

Do not force a selection. If no market qualifies, show:

“No strong paper bet today. None of today’s executable markets currently meet EdgeCast’s quality and edge requirements.”

Use Kalshi-style plain language.

Example layout:

Best paper bet today

Market:
Denver high temperature — 96°F or above

EdgeCast paper position:
Buy NO at 63¢

What that means:
EdgeCast estimates a 78% chance the temperature will stay below 96°F. A winning NO contract settles at $1.

Estimated advantage:
12 percentage points after estimated fees

Why EdgeCast likes it:
The current forecast is below the threshold, the market quote is fresh and executable, and the model’s uncertainty estimate supports a higher probability of NO than the market price implies.
